## What is this?

The Alkmaar Admin Panel gives your staff one place to moderate from. It runs
inside FiveM as an in-game panel, and the same panel is reachable from a
browser, so you can handle a report from your phone without joining the server.

## Quick start

1. [Install the resource](/installation) and import the database
2. Add your licence to `server.cfg`
3. Make yourself staff with `setstaff`
4. Press **F6** in game

New here? Start with the [installation guide](/installation).

Something not working? The [troubleshooting page](/troubleshooting) covers the
messages you are most likely to see.
